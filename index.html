Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to start an interior design project?

Begin with a clear spatial plan and a material palette. Define how you want each room to feel, then layer in lighting and furniture. Avoid buying pieces before the layout is resolved.

How do I choose the right lighting for a living room?

Use three layers: ambient (ceiling or cove), task (reading lamps), and accent (wall washers or picture lights). Dimmers are essential for flexibility.

Are sustainable materials more expensive?

Not always. Reclaimed wood, lime plaster, and local stone can be cost-competitive. The real cost is in labor and detailing — but longevity often outweighs upfront savings.

What's the biggest mistake in small-space design?

Pushing all furniture against walls. Floating pieces and defining zones with rugs and lighting actually makes a room feel larger and more intentional.

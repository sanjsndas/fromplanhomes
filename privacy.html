Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2025 Effective: January 15, 2025

At NICHE Interior Design & Architecture Journal ("NICHE", "we", "us", or "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website www.nichejournal.com (the "Site") or interact with our editorial content, newsletters, and services.

In short: We only collect what we need to run a thoughtful design journal. We never sell your personal data. You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing privacy@nichejournal.com.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect through the Site, via email, and through any related services, newsletters, or events. By using the Site, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use the Site.

We are based in Milan, Italy, and comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as well as applicable local laws. We aim to be transparent about our data practices and to give you meaningful control over your information.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

Information you provide voluntarily

  • Contact forms: When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message content.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address and any preferences you share.
  • Editorial pitches: If you pitch a story, we collect your name, email, portfolio links, and pitch content.
  • Comments and interactions: If we enable comments or interactive features, we may collect your name, email, and comment content.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • IP address (anonymized where required by law)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages viewed, time spent, and referral URLs
  • Device type and screen resolution

This data is used in aggregate to improve our content and user experience. We do not use it to identify individual visitors.

3. How we use your data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries: We use your contact details to reply to messages, pitches, and advertising requests.
  • To send newsletters: If you opt in, we use your email to send editorial updates, new articles, and occasional announcements. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To improve the Site: We analyze anonymized usage data to understand what content resonates and how to improve navigation and performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations: We may process your data to comply with applicable laws, regulations, or legal requests.
  • To protect our rights: We may use data to enforce our terms, prevent fraud, and protect the safety of our users and our publication.

We never sell your personal data. We do not share your email or personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on the Site. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. For detailed information about the cookies we use and how to manage them, please see our Cookies Policy.

You can set your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, some features of the Site may not function properly without cookies.

5. Third-party services

We may use trusted third-party service providers to help us operate the Site and deliver our services. These providers may have access to your personal information only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

Categories of third-party services we may use include:

  • Email service providers: For delivering newsletters and managing contact form submissions.
  • Analytics providers: For understanding how visitors use the Site (e.g., privacy-focused analytics).
  • Hosting and infrastructure: For storing data and serving the Site securely.
  • Social media platforms: If you interact with our social media links or embedded content.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you encounter through the Site.

6. Data retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

  • Contact inquiries: Retained for up to 2 years after our last correspondence, unless you request deletion earlier.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Retained until you unsubscribe, plus a short period to honor your request.
  • Anonymized analytics: Retained indefinitely in aggregate form, as it does not identify individuals.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Right to restrict processing: Request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability: Request transfer of your data to another organization in a structured format.
  • Right to object: Object to our processing of your data for certain purposes.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@nichejournal.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

8. Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Access controls and authentication for internal systems
  • Regular security reviews and updates
  • Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

The Site is not intended for children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, by additional notice (such as a banner on the Site or an email notification).

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.

11. Contact us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@nichejournal.com
  • Postal address: NICHE Editorial Studio, Via dei Materiali 14, 20121 Milan, Italy

We are committed to resolving any complaints or concerns you may have about your privacy. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Thank you for trusting NICHE with your attention. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data with care and respect.

Quick answers about privacy

Do you sell my email or personal data?

No. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. Your data is used solely to operate NICHE and communicate with you about our editorial content and services.

How do I unsubscribe from your newsletter?

Every newsletter includes a clear "unsubscribe" link at the bottom. You can also email us at privacy@nichejournal.com and we'll remove you from our list promptly.

What data do you collect from visitors?

We collect anonymized analytics (pages viewed, time on site) and any information you voluntarily provide via our contact form or newsletter signup. We do not collect sensitive personal data.

Can I request deletion of my data?

Yes. Email privacy@nichejournal.com with your request and we will delete your personal data within 30 days, subject to any legal retention obligations.

Cookies Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2025 Effective: January 15, 2025

This Cookies Policy explains how NICHE Interior Design & Architecture Journal ("NICHE", "we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website www.nichejournal.com (the "Site"). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

In short: We use minimal, privacy-respecting cookies to make the Site work and to understand how readers engage with our content. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

1. Introduction

When you visit the Site, we may store or retrieve information on your browser, mostly in the form of cookies. This information might be about you, your preferences, or your device, and is used to make the Site work as you expect it to, as well as to provide us with insights that help us improve your reading experience.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the technologies we use. This policy describes the categories of cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can manage them.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by a service we use).

Similar technologies include web beacons, pixels, and local storage. We refer to all of these collectively as "cookies" in this policy.

3. How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential functionality: To ensure the Site loads correctly, remembers your preferences, and keeps you logged in (if applicable).
  • Analytics and performance: To understand how visitors interact with our content — which articles are most read, how long readers stay, and how they navigate — so we can improve our editorial offerings.
  • Personalization: To remember your settings, such as your preferred reading mode or region, where applicable.

We do not use cookies for advertising or retargeting purposes, and we do not sell any data collected through cookies.

We keep our cookie use minimal and strive to select providers that respect user privacy by default (such as Plausible Analytics, which does not use cookies that track individuals across sites).

5. Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies, and they are subject to the respective third party's privacy and cookie policies. Examples include:

  • Embedded content: If we embed videos (e.g., Vimeo, YouTube) or social media posts (e.g., Instagram, X), these platforms may set their own cookies.
  • Font delivery: We use Google Fonts to serve typography; Google may collect anonymized usage data.
  • Analytics: Our analytics provider (Plausible) does not set cookies that identify individuals, but we list it here for transparency.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you encounter while browsing the Site.

6. Managing cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies or to alert you when a cookie is being set. The "Help" section of your browser will provide instructions. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Site.
  • Opt-out tools: For analytics cookies, you can use browser extensions or opt-out mechanisms provided by the analytics provider (though our analytics provider does not track individuals).
  • Consent banner: Where required by law, we may present a cookie consent banner on your first visit. You can adjust your choices at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the Site.

To learn more about cookies and how to manage them, you may visit www.allaboutcookies.org.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice (such as a banner on the Site).

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our use of cookies.

Quick answers about cookies

Do you use cookies for advertising?

No. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not permit third-party ad networks to track you on our Site. Our cookies are limited to essential functionality and privacy-respecting analytics.

Can I use NICHE without cookies?

Yes, you can browse most of the Site with cookies disabled. However, some features (like remembering your preferences) may not work as intended.

How do I delete cookies from my browser?

You can clear cookies through your browser's settings. The exact steps vary by browser — look for "Privacy" or "History" settings, then choose to clear browsing data including cookies.

Do you track me across other websites?

No. We do not use cross-site tracking technologies. Our analytics provider (Plausible) is designed to be privacy-friendly and does not track individuals across sites.

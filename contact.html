Before you write — quick answers

Do you accept guest posts or pitches?

Yes, we welcome pitches from designers, architects, and writers. Please send a short outline (200 words) and a link to your portfolio or previous work to editors@nichejournal.com. We respond to every pitch within 48 hours.

How can I advertise on NICHE?

We offer sponsored articles, newsletter placements, and display advertising. Email ads@nichejournal.com for our current media kit and rates. We only work with brands that align with our editorial values.

Can I republish your articles?

We allow limited republishing with attribution and a link back to the original. Please contact us for permission before reposting full articles. Short excerpts with credit are always welcome.

Do you review products or materials?

We do not accept free products in exchange for coverage. If you have a material or product our readers should know about, send a press release and high-res images — we'll consider it for editorial coverage.

About Us

We believe good design
starts with a plan.

From Plan Homes is an independent editorial journal exploring the intersection of interior design, architecture, and the way we live. We publish in-depth articles, practical guides, and critical perspectives — no fluff, no sponsored content.

Our story

From Plan Homes began as a simple question: why is so much design writing either shallow trend-spotting or impenetrable academic jargon? We wanted a publication that treated residential design with the seriousness it deserves — without losing the joy and practicality that makes it matter.

Founded in 2019 by architect and writer Elena Marchetti, we've grown into a trusted resource for designers, architects, and curious homeowners. Every article is researched, written, and edited by people who have actually specified materials, drawn lighting plans, and navigated the messy realities of construction.

We're proudly independent. We don't accept sponsored posts, we don't publish AI-generated filler, and we don't chase trends for the sake of clicks. Our only obligation is to our readers — and to the idea that thoughtful design can make ordinary life a little better.

What we stand for

Editorial integrity

No sponsored content, no pay-to-play. Our recommendations are based on research, experience, and a genuine belief in the value of good design.

Practical depth

We go beyond pretty pictures. Our articles explain the how and why — so you can apply what you learn, whether you're a professional or a homeowner.

Long-term thinking

We champion materials and methods that age gracefully, perform well, and tread lightly. Sustainability isn't a trend for us — it's a baseline.
